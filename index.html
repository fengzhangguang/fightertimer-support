About FighterTimer

FighterTimer is a simple vibration reminder app for iPhone. It is designed for quiet reminders during office naps, study sessions, focus time, and short breaks.

The app uses local notifications and device vibration to remind users when a timer ends.

Common Questions

Why does FighterTimer need notification permission?

FighterTimer uses local notifications to remind you when a timer ends.

Does FighterTimer collect personal data?

No. FighterTimer does not collect, store, or share personal information.

Does FighterTimer require an account?

No. FighterTimer does not require account registration or login.

Why may vibration behavior differ between devices?

Vibration behavior may depend on iPhone model, iOS settings, notification permissions, Focus mode, Silent Mode, Low Power Mode, and iOS system restrictions.

Can FighterTimer replace the built-in iPhone alarm?

FighterTimer is designed for quiet vibration reminders. It is not intended to replace emergency alarms or critical alerts.
